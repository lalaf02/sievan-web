## Outstanding findings and limitations

- **Build/integration blocker:** no Supabase credentials, so live data refresh, Storage parity, auth/RLS and production behavior remain unverified. Snapshot gates verify committed data and local media only. No pipeline-generated archive changes were mixed into the fixes.
- **P2, prospective pipeline risk — `scripts/supabase.mjs:selectAll`:** the implementation uses one response without pagination. A relation exceeding the server row cap could silently truncate. Current committed record counts do not demonstrate that failure, and live counts/configuration were unavailable. Follow up with a paginated, stable-order integration test before larger ingestion.
- **P2, prospective pipeline risk — `scripts/media.mjs:downloadMedia`:** an existing file is reused when byte size matches (or manifest size is absent). A same-size replacement could stay stale. No such replacement was established in this snapshot; validate with Storage metadata/checksums before changing the synchronization contract.
- **P3, documentation drift:** older frontend/verification prose still describes a four-section home layout, old DataModel workflows and historical page floors. The current home has two principal sections; the current design contract takes precedence. The report corrects actual counts without redesigning the current home to match older prose.
- **Existing curatorial backlog, not new defects:** zero finished-painting catalogue entries and scholarship entries; gallery checklist transcription; boxes 3–6; missing OCR and transcript time alignment; all seven interviews undated; unreviewed article records. The snapshot confirms 51/60 articles unreviewed, 41/60 below high parse confidence, 25 without headlines and 23 without bylines. These records were not rewritten.
- **Coverage boundaries:** Chromium on this Mac, not Safari/Firefox or physical phones; no screen-reader session or exhaustive contrast audit of pixels inside scanned media; no historical authentication of every archival assertion. Empty painting paths received source review and date utility regression coverage, not a populated end-to-end fixture export. Responsive states represent every layout family, not every combination of every filter on every record. External destinations and email delivery were not exercised.

The retained archival distinctions are central: 76 physical objects include 25 sheets and 51 documentary objects; 55 objects have imagery and 21 do not; 60 press notices belong to 30 publications; 29 people, 15 exhibitions and 25 places remain. Evidence grades remain separate, inferred relationships remain qualified, and deliberately absent material remains visible.
