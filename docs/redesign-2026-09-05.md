# Sievan whole-app editorial redesign

**Date:** 5 September 2026  
**Reference:** Enfant gâté, translated rather than reproduced  
**Baseline:** the reviewed and corrected local worktree documented in `review-2026-09-05.md`

## Result

The public archive now opens as a warm reading room. The system carries the reference site's confidence through scale, vermilion, condensed display type, open composition, and shaped editorial media while preserving Sievan's archival structure and factual distinctions. Dark mode remains a coordinated charcoal and vermilion peer.

No routes, record identifiers, data interfaces, catalogue grades, archive facts, or production credential requirements changed.

## Shared system

The source package in `../sievan-design` was changed first and synced into `design/`. The redesign introduces:

- a warm paper light default and explicit-cookie dark preference;
- Roboto Condensed for navigation, labels, display headings, dates, and counts;
- Source Serif 4 for archival prose, quotation, transcript, and object description;
- Inter for controls, filters, and compact metadata;
- larger responsive display tokens, broader page measures, vermilion structural rules, and lighter surface depth;
- shared page-introduction, alternating-band, count, notice, arched-media, and circular-media primitives;
- contrast checks for both the default and explicit dark token blocks.

All checked text pairs meet 4.5:1 and all checked control or structural boundaries meet 3:1 in both themes.

## Page families

- **Shell:** slim sticky paper header at desktop widths, condensed uppercase destinations, compact search, quiet theme control, and wrapped mobile navigation with search kept visible.
- **Homepage:** oversized name and date composition, offset arched footage, open editorial copy, and four section entrances built from scale and rules rather than cards.
- **Life:** asymmetric introduction, oversized section markers, arched estate footage, looser chronology and testimony rhythm, and a less table-like retrospective presentation.
- **Works:** prominent five-period graphic spine, separate dated and evidence counts, open period groups, and larger catalogue contact sheets.
- **Archive and indexes:** expressive counts, quieter filter tools, open editorial result lists and contact sheets, and stronger typographic grouping across press, publications, people, places, and exhibitions.
- **Records and transcripts:** oversized record identity, media-led object pages, compact ruled facts, Source Serif reading measure, and subdued sticky transcript navigation.
- **Absence and overlays:** intentional absence uses an oxide-tinted dashed evidence state; catalogue dialogs use a vermilion top edge and retain native keyboard behavior.

Document scans and artwork reproductions remain rectangular, contained, and uncropped. Arches are limited to editorial footage. Motion remains limited to short state changes, with reduced-motion behavior preserved.
